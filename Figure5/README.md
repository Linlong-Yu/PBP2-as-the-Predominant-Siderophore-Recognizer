## Scripts

* **siderophore_PBP2_scatter.R** Analyzes the correlation between the number of siderophore BGCs and the number of PBP2 genes within each genome.
(Corresponds to **Figure 5d e f**.)
* **SBP_distribution_all_siderophoreBGCs.R** Profiles the global distribution and frequency of all Substrate-Binding Proteins (SBPs) associated with siderophore BGCs across the entire dataset.
* **PBP2_siderophorebgc_number_distribution.R** Examines the quantitative abundance and frequency distribution of PBP2 genes and siderophore BGCs across different bacterial strains and genera.
(Corresponds to **Figure a** and **b**.)
* **strain_noPBP2.R** Identifies and extracts specific bacterial strains that completely lack PBP2 homologs, facilitating the investigation of alternative iron-uptake mechanisms.
* **BGC_PBP2_ratio.R** Calculates and compares the distribution ratio of PBP2 genes located within siderophore BGCs versus other types of BGCs to evaluate functional enrichment.
(Corresponds to **Figure 4c**.)
